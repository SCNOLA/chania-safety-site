These sites block being embedded inline (they send an X-Frame-Options header that stops any other site from framing them) — that's their own security setting, not something this page can override, so they open in a new tab instead. NASA FIRMS also offers a real hotspot-data layer via a personal (free) MAP_KEY — let me know if you want that wired in later.

Emergency Contacts (Greece — national numbers)

112
EU Emergency / Civil Protection
Fires, evacuations, any life-threatening emergency. Multilingual.
199
Fire Service (Πυροσβεστική)
Report a fire directly to the fire brigade.
166
EKAB — Ambulance
National Center for Emergency Care, medical emergencies.
100
Police (Αστυνομία)
Police emergency line.

These are standard nationwide numbers, valid anywhere in Greece including Chania. This page does not have verified local station numbers — dial one of the above for anything local.

Not an official emergency service. Built as a quick reference — always defer to 112 / Greek Civil Protection announcements.

Emergency Contacts (Greece — national numbers)

Fires, evacuations, any life-threatening emergency. Multilingual.

Fire Service (Πυροσβεστική)
Report a fire directly to the fire brigade.
166
EKAB — Ambulance
National Center for Emergency Care, medical emergencies.
100
Police (Αστυνομία)
Police emergency line.

These are standard nationwide numbers, valid anywhere in Greece including Chania. This page does not have verified local station numbers — dial one of the above for anything local.

Live satellite fire hotspots (VIIRS, last 24h) from NASA FIRMS, centered on Chania. Each marker is a detected thermal anomaly — not every one is a wildfire (can include controlled burns, industrial heat sources, or false positives), and a lack of markers doesn't guarantee there's no fire, only that satellites haven't flagged one in the last pass.
